## Handling a matter

1. **Intake.** A report, a submission, a source's word or a Curator's own observation is logged the same hour, with time, place and who reported it.
2. **Collection entry.** Person or matter, what was seen or heard, date, place, witnesses, the material gathered, and the Curator's name. Entries record; they never accuse.
3. **Assessment.** The Curator asks one question: does this disclose a spiritual offence, need a coercive measure, or neither? Anything that turns on what the Doctrine means is marked a question of meaning and goes up untouched.
4. **Outcome.** Kept on the Collection and watched; or referred to the Advocate for adjudication; or referred to the Office for a coercive measure.
5. **Follow-up.** The Curator records what the Advocate decided and carries out any direction to the Department without reinterpreting it.

The Department itself only ever reaches the middle row; any measure of force sits with another agency.

## Prior review (s.6)

- Submissions are answered within one day: **permit**, **permit with alterations** (each alteration listed), or **withhold**.
- A withheld work is withdrawn everywhere: shelves, programmes, curricula, catalogues. The Department checks that it was.
- The submitting office keeps the fact of submission confidential; a breach is noted and referred.

## Referral package

Every referral to the Advocate carries: the facts, the Collection entries relied on, the material gathered, witnesses, the provision of the Spiritual Penal Code or mandate engaged, and any question of meaning stated plainly for him to answer. A referral for a coercive measure also names which agency is needed and why: the DA to compel NSB, ABI or NYPD, or a VSK Tasking through the Security Liaison.

## Disposition ladder (Seditious Terminology Mandate)

A first public utterance is answered by a caution or a fine up to DM 1,000; a second within a week, or one before superiors, goes to the Title 70 register. The Department recommends; the Office decides.

| Occasion | Recommendation | Who decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First public utterance | Caution, or fine up to DM 1,000 | The Office |
| Second within a week | Title 70 register | The Office |
| Any utterance before superiors or command | Title 70 register | The Office |
| Bad-faith or score-settling report | Reporter referred as a Title 70 matter | The Office |
