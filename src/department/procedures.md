## Handling a matter

1. **Intake.** A report, a submission, a source's word or a Curator's own observation is logged the same hour, with time, place and who reported it.
2. **Collection entry.** Person or matter, what was seen or heard, date, place, witnesses, the material gathered, and the Curator's name. Entries record; they never accuse.
3. **Assessment.** The Curator asks one question: does this disclose a spiritual offence, need a coercive measure, or neither? Anything that turns on what the Doctrine means is marked a question of meaning and goes up untouched.
4. **Outcome.** Kept on the Collection and watched; or referred to the Advocate for adjudication; or referred to the Office for a coercive measure.
5. **Follow-up.** The Curator records what was decided and carries out any direction to the Department without reinterpreting it.

The Department itself only ever reaches the middle row; any measure of force sits with another agency, on a warrant.

## Questions of meaning

A question of meaning is any question of what the Doctrine, Providence or the Imperial Will requires, or whether a person, act, writing or measure conforms to it (Spiritual Penal Law §10.00). A Curator never answers one. He submits it in writing to the Director: the facts, the decision at issue and the question, signed with name and office. The Director rules under the Advocate's authority or sends it up to the Deputy Public Advocate or the Public Advocate. Their ruling is final. Pending an answer, the Curator does not act on the point in question.

## Prior review (s.6)

Every office that licenses, funds, houses, prints or issues cultural matter submits it before release. Submissions are answered within one day with one of three dispositions:

| Disposition | Form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Permit** | C-STAMP | Conforms to the Doctrine and the Third Work. Stamped and archived in the master registry. |
| **Demand alteration** | C-REVISE | Minor ambiguity or unapproved tone. The form lists every page and line to be cut. Release is barred until re-inspection. |
| **Withhold** | C-BAN | Seditious content, Republic nostalgia or a spiritual fault. Manuscripts, printing plates and broadcast tapes are impounded into the Collection. |

- A withheld work is withdrawn everywhere: shelves, programmes, curricula, catalogues. The Department checks that it was.
- The submitting office keeps the fact of submission confidential. Revealing that a review is under way, or leaking a disposition, is hindering a spiritual inquiry (§130.05, a class III felony) and costs the licence.
- Refusing or delaying records a Curator requires is obstructing a spiritual inquiry (§130.00).

## Field identification of spiritual offences

Observations are weighed against the Spiritual Penal Law. The archetypes are the Third Work's.

| Offence | Archetype | What it looks like in the field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blasphemy, §100 | The Undertaker | Denying or mocking the Emperor's eternity; treating the Crown as mortal; plans or papers that assume a vacant Crown |
| Glorification of the former Republic, §105.05 | The Mourner | Republic insignia displayed; old holidays kept; the Fourth, a presidential portrait, a flag in the cedar chest |
| Denial of the surrender, §105.00 | The Peacebreaker | Calling the Protectorate an occupation or its government unlawful |
| Spiritual sedition, §105.10 | — | Publishing, addressing an assembly or organizing to restore the Republic |
| Unlawful reservation of authority, §110 | The Holdout | A public servant keeping files, rooms or customs from the Office; “home rule” claimed against the Party |
| Promoting self-government, §115 | The Candleman | Agitating for elections, plebiscites, a free press or private judgment; citing Republic instruments as authority |
| Heresy, §120 | The Heretic | Declaring what the Imperial Will requires without the Office's words; justifying an order by “what the Emperor wants” |
| Official estrangement, §140 | The Tourist and the Carrier | Governing from a distance without knowing the population; bringing Republic habits back into an office |

## The referral package (Form C-DOSSIER)

Every referral carries: the facts in order and dated; the Collection entries relied on, by number; the material gathered; witnesses and how each may be reached; the provision of the Spiritual Penal Law or mandate engaged; and any question of meaning stated plainly. The Curator transmits it to the desk of the Director.

The Director reviews the file, drafts the indictment (Form PA-SPC-01), applies the modifiers of §65.10 and §65.20, and attaches the “cracked beam” charge of perpetual disqualification (§60.10) where the offence warrants it.

## Coercive measures

No search, seizure or arrest is made in aid of a spiritual inquiry except by a law enforcement agency on a judicial warrant (Spiritual Penal Law §200.15; Constitution Amendment II). Where one is needed, the Department issues Form PA-DA-01 to the District Attorney, who is compelled under Home Rule Act s.10 to apply for the warrant and to order the NYPD, ABI or NSB to execute it. Protection, surveillance support or custody in a Title 70 matter is requested as a Tasking of the Security Forces, through the Advocate.
