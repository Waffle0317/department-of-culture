An offence committed on a Day of Providence, or at any observance of the Party, is raised one class (Spiritual Penal Law §65.10). Failing to keep a Day as the Office directs, including leaving a window unlit where lighting is directed, is neglect of observance (§150.00).

| Observance | Date | Who attends | What is checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day of Revelation | 25 December | Every desk | Observances as directed; windows lit where directed; speeches as delivered |
| Consecration Day | 22 January | Every desk; the head at the principal observance | Oath-takings; who keeps the Day and who does not |
| Miracle Day | 14 February | Public Bodies desk | Observances of offices and commands |
| Day of Peace | 15 April | Every desk | The surrender kept, not mourned; any Republic insignia or old holiday observed (§105.05) |
| Watch Night | 18 April | Every desk; the head at the principal observance | Windows lit; attendance of offices; conduct and speech before command |
| Day of the Honours | First Monday of September | Public Bodies desk; Stage desk | Order of honours as published; Recognitions; press coverage before and after; the trades' observance under the Workers' Advocacy |
| Day of the Storm | 29 September | Public Bodies desk | Observances of commands and garrison |
| Deutscher Tag | First Sunday of October | Stage desk with the Germanic Desk | The parade, bands, choirs and the Honour Guard of the Party; the beer gardens and halls after |
| Party observances | As proclaimed | Public Bodies desk | Attendance of public servants; words spoken before command |

## Before each observance

1. Programmes and texts submitted under s.6 and answered.
2. Curators assigned, declared or undeclared, and a pair named for each venue.
3. A Collection entry opened for the observance itself, to hold what is seen.

## After each observance

1. Entries completed the same night and signed.
2. Anything heard before superiors or command marked for the Title 70 recommendation.
3. A line for the weekly report.
