## The forms of the Field Manual

| Form | Used for | Issued by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C-STAMP | Permit: affixed to approved matter and archived in the master registry | Curator |
| C-REVISE | Demand alteration: every page and line to be cut; release barred until re-inspection | Curator |
| C-BAN | Withhold: the work is banned and its manuscripts, plates and tapes impounded into the Collection | Curator |
| C-DOSSIER | Referral: observation logs and source statements from the Collection, to the Director | Curator |
| PA-SPC-01 | Indictment under the Spiritual Penal Law, with the modifiers of §65 and the §60.10 charge where warranted | Director |
| PA-DA-01 | To the District Attorney: compels a warrant application and execution by NYPD, ABI or NSB | Director |

The templates below are the Department's working sheets behind those forms.

## Two-hour report (Art. V)

For public servants. One sheet, filed within two hours of the words being heard.

<div class="callout typewriter">
REPORT UNDER ART. V &nbsp;·&nbsp; Seditious Terminology Mandate<br>
Reporting servant (name, office): ____________________<br>
Date and time heard: ________ &nbsp; Place: ________________<br>
Words as spoken: ___________________________________<br>
Spoken by: ____________ &nbsp; In the presence of: ____________<br>
Before superiors or command? &nbsp;☐ No &nbsp;☐ Yes<br>
Filed: ________ (within two hours: ☐ Yes ☐ No, reason: ______)
</div>

A good-faith report carries no penalty. A false report is a Title 70 matter.

## Referral package (Form C-DOSSIER)

To the Director, for the Advocate. Nothing goes up without every line.

1. The facts, in order, dated.
2. The Collection entries relied on, by number.
3. The material gathered, listed and attached.
4. Witnesses, with how each may be reached.
5. The provision of the Spiritual Penal Code or mandate engaged.
6. Any question of meaning, stated plainly for the Advocate to answer.
7. For a coercive measure: which agency is needed and why (the DA to compel NSB, ABI or NYPD, or a VSK Tasking through the Security Liaison).
8. Final review by the head, signed.

## Weekly report to the Advocate

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Matters reviewed | Submissions received; permitted; permitted with alterations; withheld |
| Collection | New entries by desk; entries closed |
| Referrals | Made this week, to whom, status of each |
| Reports received | Count by channel: Art. V, Box 70, sources, Wardens, Germanic Desk |
| Questions of meaning | Each stated plainly, awaiting the Advocate |
| Terminology | Terms proposed for the banned list, with the words that prompted them |
| The Days | Observances attended; what was checked; what was seen |
| Discipline | Faults recorded; lessons; referrals |
