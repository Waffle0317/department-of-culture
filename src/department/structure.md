The Department of Culture is established within the Office of the Public Advocate (Mandate s.4) as the corrective arm of the Advocacy (Proclamation of 12 October 1962). Its officers are the Public Curators, appointed by the Advocate and answering to him alone; its register is the Collection; and its head is the Director of Culture, who answers to the Public Advocate through the Deputy Public Advocate.

## The four desks

| Desk | Covers | Main sources of material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Print and Broadcast | Newspapers, books, leaflets, radio, television, film | s.6 submissions from the Department of Media and licensing offices; newsstands; Box 70 |
| Stage, Music and Societies | Theatres, clubs, music halls, choirs, Turnvereine, social clubs, veterans' halls | Attendance at performances and meetings; Germanic Desk contacts; confidential sources |
| Schools and Instruction | Classrooms, lectures, textbooks, sermons, Workers' Advocacy instruction | Curriculum submissions; attendance at classes; Wardens' reports |
| Public Bodies and Observances | Conduct and speech inside offices, Council, precincts and commands; the Days and Party observances | Two-hour reports from public servants; attendance at Council and observances |

## What the head keeps personally

The head of the Department keeps three things personally: the Collection, the roll of sources, and every referral's final review before it reaches the Advocate. Curators work in German–American pairs wherever numbers allow.

## Curators

- Appointed by and answering to the Advocate alone (s.9).
- Sworn by the Oath of the Second Surrender; enrolled on the Party's Central Membership Registry.
- Hold no other office of the Capital.
- Know only their own sources; the roll is held by the Office.
- Faults judged in heightened degree. One recorded mistake is a lesson; two is a referral.

## Divisions of the Department

Every Curator sits in one of three divisions, and every file on the Collection is read under one of the same three compartments. A Curator's clearance is set per compartment by the Director in the admin hub.

| Division | Holds files on | Organizations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | The open life of the Capital | NYPD, civilians |
| Secret | The security services and the Capital's garrison | VSK (Volkssicherheitskräfte), NSB, ABI, 1st Military Police Battalion, 11th Division (National Guard), 91st Air Division "Himmelwolf" (Honor Guard of the Lord Protector) |
| Government | The Governor's office, the Council of New York, the civil service and the Party | Government; the Party (Tier 4 only) |

Within a division, Tier 1 reads enlisted and NCO files, Tier 2 adds officers, Tier 3 adds general officers and government, and Tier 4 reads everything in every compartment.
