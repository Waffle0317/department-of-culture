Abt. II and Abt. III run placements inside agencies together: Abt. II issues the cover, Abt. III handles the Curator. Each division's handbook is its own standing order and follows.

## The three divisions

| Division | Lane | Watches | Works by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Abteilung I · Regierung** | Government · in the open | The Governor, the Mayor, the Council of New York; Council sessions; public texts; the conduct of officials | Attendance and record. Declared where useful; the Office's hand is seen |
| **Abteilung II · Schatten** | Secret · anonymous, unseen | Anyone, anything: the clubs, halls, congregations, pressrooms and streets of the Capital | Informants under cipher; cover identities; detention on the Court's order and no one's else |
| **Abteilung III · Öffentlichkeit** | Public · inside the agencies | VSK, NYPD, ABI, NSB and the Army's formations in the Capital | Liaisons and placed Curators inside each agency; carries the Department's requests for force to the Court or the District Attorney |

The Collection is compartmented on the same lanes: files on the agencies and the garrison are read under Öffentlichkeit (public), files on civilians under Schatten (secret), and files on the Government and the Party under Regierung (government).

## Ranks and shared rules

Every member of the Department is a Public Curator first; rank sets who directs whom, not who answers to the Advocate.

| Rank | English | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kulturdirektor** | Director of Culture | Heads the Department; keeps the Collection, the rolls and final review of every referral |
| **Stellvertretender Kulturdirektor** | Deputy Director of Culture | Stands in for the Director; directs the division chiefs in his absence |
| **Abteilungsleiter** | Division Chief | Leads one division; reports to the Director weekly |
| **Oberkurator** | Senior Curator | Leads a desk or a team; trains new Curators |
| **Kurator** | Curator | Field officer of the Department |
| **Anwärter** | Candidate | On probation; works only beside a Kurator until appointed |

## Authority

Most of what the divisions do already rests on the Mandate on Culture and the Spiritual Penal Code.

| Power | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Watch all cultural matter, including the conduct and speech of public bodies | Mandate on Culture s.3, s.5 |
| Order works submitted before release; permit, alter or withhold | Mandate s.6 |
| Attend any public or admitted gathering without declaring office | Mandate s.7 |
| Keep confidential sources and the Collection | Mandate s.7 |
| Receive two-hour sedition reports from every public servant | Seditious Terminology Mandate art. V |
| Charge obstruction (class A misdemeanour) and hindering (class III felony) against anyone who blocks or warns | Spiritual Penal Code §130.00, §130.05 |
| Raise the class of any offence committed in defiance of a Curator | Spiritual Penal Code §65.10 |
| Execute the process of the Court of Public Order, which may summon, arrest and detain | Court of Public Order Mandate art. 3 |
