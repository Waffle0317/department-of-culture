## Powers and limits

| Area | What the Department may do | What it may not do | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nature | Exercise the Office's power to investigate under Home Rule Act s.6 | Exercise any other power | Mandate on Culture s.4 |
| Jurisdiction | Supervise all cultural matter: print, broadcast, film, stage, music, schools, lectures, sermons, clubs, assemblies | Determine a question of meaning, which is reserved to the Public Advocate | s.3, s.5 |
| Prior review | Order offices that license, fund, house, print or issue cultural matter to submit it first; permit, withhold or permit with alterations | Act on matter no office handles (private works are reached by observation and referral) | s.6 |
| Investigation | Observe, gather, attend public and admitted gatherings undeclared, keep confidential sources | Search on warrant, arrest, seize | s.4, s.7 |
| Record | Keep the Collection of persons, matters and observations | Treat an entry as an accusation; open it to any other agency | s.7 |
| Referral | Refer spiritual offences to the Advocate; refer coercive needs to the Office, which may require the DA to compel NSB, ABI or NYPD | Take any coercive measure itself | s.8 |
| Court process | Execute the process of the Court of Public Order when it issues one | Arrest or detain on its own authority | Court of Public Order Mandate art. 3 |
| Staff | Curators appointed by and answering to the Advocate alone; Oath of the Second Surrender; faults judged in heightened degree | Hold any other office of the Capital | s.9 |
| Secrecy | Keep office, sources, investigations and rolls in confidence; rolls held by the Office alone | Disclose to Council, Governor, courts or other agencies without leave | s.10 |

## Instruments that feed the daily work

- **Seditious Terminology Mandate, Art. V.** Every public servant must report seditious words, insults or slurs to the Department within two hours. The Department is the intake for the whole city.
- **Determination regarding Richard Dent, s.1.** The Department is charged with securing and examining the records of the District Attorney's office for Dent's tenure.
- **The Third Work, Book Two, Part Ten.** Tells every servant to submit material before release, give Curators access, withdraw withheld works everywhere, and never warn anyone under review.

<div class="callout callout--red">
<span class="label label--red">Amendment II to the Constitution</span>
Any raid without probable cause and a signed warrant is a Class III felony. The Department will never be the office that commits it.
