<div class="callout callout--red">
<span class="label label--red">Never directly</span>
The Department never asks the NYPD, ABI or NSB for anything directly. That keeps every coercive act on the agency with the power to take it, and keeps the Department out of Amendment II.
</div>

## Asking for a Tasking

When an inquiry needs protection or surveillance support, the Curator writes the need up as part of the referral package and the Director carries it to the Advocate. The Advocate, not the Department, issues the Tasking to the Security Liaison, and a Tasking in a Title 70 matter is carried out as written and without delay. Every member of the Security Forces engaged on it keeps the matter in confidence; a warning given to anyone concerned is itself a Title 70 matter.
