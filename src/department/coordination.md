| Office | What flows to the Department | What flows back | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public Advocate | Directions, answers to questions of meaning | Weekly report, referrals | Mandate s.8–s.10 |
| Office of the Arbiter | Warrants and adjudications | Referral packages via the Advocate | Offices of the Advocacy |
| Court of Public Order | Process to execute | Execution of that process | Court mandate art. 3 |
| Department of Media | Matter submitted before release | Permit, alter or withhold | Mandate s.6 |
| Office for Information, Records, and Patents | Permit and licence data; custody of surrendered copies | Withdrawn and surrendered works | Dent Determination |
| Office for Germanic Affairs / Germanic Desk | Contacts with German-language press, choirs, societies | Nothing from the Collection | Welfare memorandum |
| Workers' Advocacy | Wardens' reports touching cultural matter or speech | Nothing from the Collection | Workers' Advocacy Order |
| Security Advocate and Liaison (VSK) | Protection, surveillance support, custody, on the Advocate's Tasking | Requests for a Tasking, through the Advocate | Security Advocate Proclamation s.5 |
| District Attorney | Compelled action by NSB, ABI or NYPD, when the Office requires it | Referral, through the Office | Home Rule Act s.10; Mandate s.8 |
| All public servants | Two-hour reports of seditious words | Protection for good-faith reports | Seditious Terminology Mandate art. V |

<div class="callout callout--red">
<span class="label label--red">Never directly</span>
The Department never asks the NYPD, ABI or NSB for anything directly. That keeps every coercive act on the agency with the power to take it, and keeps the Department out of Amendment II.
</div>

## Asking for a Tasking

When an inquiry needs protection or surveillance support, the Curator writes the need up as part of the referral package and the head carries it to the Advocate. The Advocate, not the Department, issues the Tasking to the Security Liaison.
