## Section 1: Short title

This ordinance shall be known and may be cited as the “Mandate on Culture.”

## Section 2: Declaration

1. It is hereby declared that the spiritual unity of the organs of the State with the People, and the public morality of the Capital, are essential to public order and to the Imperial authority.
2. It is further declared that such unity and morality are formed and sustained in that which the people hear, read, are taught and behold, and that the supervision is committed to the Public Advocate by section six of the New York Home Rule Act.
3. It is the purpose of this mandate to establish an instrument for the exercise of the investigative power so committed, and to provide that it can be exercised with the confidence that its effectiveness requires.

## Section 3: Definitions

1. “Department” means the Department of Culture established by section four of this mandate.
2. “Curator” means a Public Curator appointed pursuant to section ten of this mandate.
3. “Cultural matter” means any writing, printing, publication, broadcast, recording, motion picture, dramatic or musical performance, course of instruction, lecture, address, sermon, exhibition, or assembly, and any club, society or gathering wherein the spirit of the Capital is formed or influenced.
4. “Office” means the Office of the Public Advocate of New York.
5. “Question of meaning” means any question of what the Third Work, Providence or the Imperial Will requires, or whether a person, act or cultural matter conforms thereto, the determination whereof is reserved to the Public Advocate.

## Section 4: Department of Culture

1. There is hereby established, within the Office, a Department of Culture.
2. The Department shall be an investigative body. It shall exercise the power to investigate committed to the Office by section six of the New York Home Rule Act, and no other power.
3. The Department possesses power of arrest, of search upon warrant, or of seizure of person or property, all via the Court of Public Order. Where any such measure is required, the Department shall proceed by referral as provided in section nine of this mandate.

## Section 5: Jurisdiction

1. The Department shall keep under supervision the cultural matters of the Capital, to the end that spiritual offences, and the faults defined in the Doctrine which precede them, may be discovered, inquired into, and referred.
2. Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no determination of a question of meaning shall be made by any Curator, but every such question shall be reserved to the Public Advocate.

## Section 6: Prior review of cultural matters

1. Every officer or agency of the Capital that licenses, funds, houses, prints or issues any cultural matter shall, upon the direction of the Department by standing or particular order, submit the same to the Department prior to its release, publication or presentation.
2. The Department may permit any cultural matter so submitted, may withhold the same, or may permit the same upon such alteration as it shall direct. A cultural matter withheld shall not be released, published or presented within the Capital, and shall be removed from public or private access.
3. No officer or agency submitting a cultural matter under this section shall disclose the fact of its submission or the determination thereon, except so far as is necessary to give effect to such determination.

## Section 7: Conduct of investigation

1. The Department shall conduct its supervision by observation, by the gathering of cultural matters, by attendance at public and admitted gatherings, and by the cultivation and keeping of sources of information.
2. A Curator may attend any public performance, meeting, class, exhibition or observance, and any gathering to which he is admitted, and shall not be required to declare his office in so doing.
3. The Department may maintain and keep, in confidence, persons who furnish to it information concerning cultural matters; and the identity of every such person shall be protected as a record of the Department.
4. The Department shall keep a record, to be known as the “Collection,” of the persons, cultural matters and observations gathered by it. An entry in the Collection shall not constitute an accusation. The Collection shall not be open to any officer or agency other than the Office.
5. Any person who shall refuse, delay or render incomplete any production required under this section shall be guilty of the spiritual offence of obstruction; and any person who shall warn another that he is under inquiry by the Department shall be guilty of the spiritual offence.

## Section 8: Referral

1. Where an investigation discloses a spiritual offence, the Curator shall refer the matter, with the material gathered, to the Public Advocate for adjudication under the Spiritual Penal Code.
2. Every referral, and the material supporting it, shall remain confidential to the Office and to the agency to which it is directed.

## Section 9: Public Curators

1. The Public Advocate shall appoint such Public Curators as he shall require, who shall serve at his pleasure and shall answer to him alone.
2. No Curator shall hold any other office of the Capital.
3. Every Curator shall take and keep the Oath of the Second Surrender; and a fault committed by a Curator shall be adjudged in its heightened degree.
4. The conduct and discretion of every Curator shall be subject to supervision by the Office.

## Section 10: Confidentiality

1. The Department shall conduct the whole of its functions in confidence. No Curator shall disclose his office, his investigations, his sources, or the contents of the Collection, to any person other than the Office, except so far as the discharge of his duty requires and the Office permits.
2. Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the rolls of the Department, comprising the names and number of its Curators and of the persons who furnish information to it, shall be kept by the Office alone, and shall not be laid before the Council, the Governor, any court, or any agency of the State.
3. Any Curator who shall disclose the functions, sources or rolls of the Department without leave of the Office shall be guilty, trialled, and shall forfeit his office.
4. Nothing in this section shall operate against the Public Advocate, to whom every Curator shall answer wholly.

This ordinance shall take effect immediately.
